Questions We Hear from Business Owners

What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)?
AEO is the practice of structuring your website and content so that AI-powered search engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google Gemini can understand, trust, and recommend your business when users ask buying-intent questions. It goes beyond traditional SEO by targeting the new generation of AI answer engines.
How is AEO different from regular SEO?
Traditional SEO targets blue-link rankings on Google. AEO targets AI answer engines that now respond directly to queries with specific business recommendations. Standard SEO alone does not guarantee that an AI engine will recommend your business. AEO ensures it will.
Do I need to be tech-savvy to work with One Trendy Owl Digital?
Not at all. We handle everything from the initial audit to ongoing monthly reporting. You never need to write code, touch your website backend, or log into a CMS. Our service is 100% done-for-you.
How much does the AI Search Audit cost?
The AI Search Audit starts at $99. It gives you a clear picture of exactly where you stand in AI search results and a specific action plan for what needs to change. There is no obligation to purchase any additional services after the audit.
Is there a long-term contract?
No long-term contracts are required. You can start with a $99 audit and see the value before committing to any ongoing services. We believe results should earn your continued business, not a contract.
How quickly will I see results?
Your AI Search Audit gives you a clear, prioritized action plan within 1 business day of booking - showing exactly what's holding your AI visibility back and what to fix first. The audit itself is the fast part; how quickly your AI visibility actually improves depends on your website, industry, and how much optimization is needed.
