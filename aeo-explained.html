How AI Decides

How AI Search Engines Decide Who to Recommend

Traditional search engines rank ten websites on a page and let the user decide. AI search engines work differently. When someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google Gemini a question like "What is the best accountant near me?" or "Which e-commerce platform should I use for a small store?", the AI gives a single answer or a short list of specific names.

To decide which businesses to include in that answer, AI engines look at four key signals on your website and across the web. If those signals are weak or missing, you do not appear. That is true no matter how good your service actually is.

Answer Engine Optimization is the practice of making sure every one of those signals is strong, clear, and correctly formatted so AI engines can read, trust, and recommend your business.

Structured data on your websiteSchema markup that tells AI engines exactly who you are, what you do, and where you are located.
Clarity of your contentAnswer-ready pages that directly address the questions buyers actually ask AI engines.
Authority signalsReviews, citations, and third-party mentions that confirm your business is legitimate and trusted.
Consistency across the webYour name, address, and details matching perfectly everywhere AI engines gather data from.

AEO vs. Traditional SEO: What Is the Difference?

SEO and AEO share the same goal: get your business in front of the right people. But they work at different layers of the web, and you need both.

Common Questions

Questions We Hear from Small Business Owners

Do I need to rebuild my website to get AEO benefits?
No. We work within your existing site and make targeted improvements without disrupting what you already have. In most cases, AEO improvements are additions to your current site, not a replacement of it. No redesign, no new platform required.
How quickly will I see results from AEO?
AI search visibility typically improves within 4 to 8 weeks of implementing structured data and content changes, though timelines vary by industry and how competitive your niche is. Your audit will show you a prioritized list of changes with the fastest expected impact.
Is AEO just for tech companies or large brands?
Not at all. Local plumbers, dentists, consultants, and e-commerce stores all benefit from AI recommendations, often more than large brands. When someone asks an AI engine "Find me a plumber in Bristol," a small well-optimized local business can appear ahead of a national chain because its local signals are stronger.

Will AEO replace my existing SEO work?
No. AEO builds on your existing SEO foundation. We add the structured data and content layers that AI engines specifically need. Think of it as an upgrade to your existing strategy, not a replacement. Your Google rankings are not affected negatively by AEO work.
Do I need to know anything technical to get started?
Nothing at all. We handle every technical step, including schema markup, structured data, and chatbot setup, from start to finish. You will never need to write code, log into your CMS back-end, or understand the technical details. We send you a plain-English report when each step is complete.
