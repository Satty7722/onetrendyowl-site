Service 02

Schema Markup and Structured Data Setup

Schema markup is the language AI engines use to understand who you are, what you do, and why you should be recommended. Most small business websites have none of it, or have it set up incorrectly.

We implement the right schema types for your business category, test every tag, and verify that AI engines can read your data clearly and confidently.

  • Business, product, service, and FAQ schema implemented correctly the first time
  • Structured data validated against Google, Bing, and AI engine standards
  • Ongoing monitoring so schema does not break when your site updates
  • No coding required from you at any stage

FAQ

Questions About Our Services

What is included in the AI Search Audit?
The audit covers a full review of how AI engines read your site, schema markup and structured data gap analysis, content structure assessment for AI answer eligibility, and a prioritized fix list with jargon-free explanations. It starts at $99 with no long-term commitment.
Do I need to do any technical work myself?
No. Every service is 100% done-for-you. You never need to write code, touch your CMS, or configure anything technical. We handle the entire process from start to finish.
Which service should I start with?
Most clients start with the AI Search Audit at $99. It tells you exactly where you stand and what to fix first. From there you can decide whether to handle fixes yourself or hand them to us for our Schema Setup or Full AEO Overhaul service.
Can I get just the schema markup without the full overhaul?
Absolutely. Schema Markup and Structured Data Setup is a standalone service. If you already have a clear picture of what needs fixing and just need implementation, we can start there directly.
How does the AI chatbot get trained on my business?
After a short onboarding session we collect information about your services, pricing, common questions, and how you prefer leads to be qualified. We handle all the training and testing before the chatbot goes live on your site.
Is there a long-term contract?
No long-term contracts are required. You can start with a $99 audit and see the value before committing to any ongoing services. We believe results should earn your continued business, not a contract.
